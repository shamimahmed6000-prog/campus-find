Campus Find help

Rules & FAQ

Quick answers to help items get safely back to their owners.

How do I post a lost or found item?

Sign in, choose “Post an item,” and add the type, description, campus location, date, and contact number. A photo is optional. Posts stay private until an admin reviews them.

When will my post appear in Browse items?

Every new post is marked Pending and is visible only to you and the admin team. It appears publicly after an admin approves it.

How do I claim an item?

Open an approved item, sign in, and submit a claim with a contact number and proof that identifies it as yours. Include details that are not already shown in the public post.

What should I include as proof?

Describe a unique mark, an item kept inside, or another detail only the owner would know. Do not share passwords, full ID numbers, or other sensitive information.

Who verifies claims, and what happens next?

An admin makes the final decision after reviewing the claim. If approved, the item is marked Returned and other pending claims for it are rejected.

Where and when can I collect a returned item?

Returned items are handed over at the Security Office, Sunday through Thursday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. Contact the admin team if you need help arranging a collection.
